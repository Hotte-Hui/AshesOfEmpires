# Canon guardrails and timeline (provisional)

## Locked
- Year: 1964, alternative Europe.
- The war ends without decisive victory by either Nazi Germany or the Soviet Union.
- Both regimes survive; Germany is partially reconstructed, Soviet industrial regions are more visibly damaged in the initial chapters.
- Berlin contains a fictional completed Great Hall; historical Volkshalle was **never built**.
- Diplomatic cooperation is fragile, not friendship between regimes.
- Both protagonists are ordinary professionals, not ideological mouthpieces.

## Provisional timeline — explicitly fictional
- 1940s–1950s: A prolonged, devastating conflict and cascading infrastructure failures.
- Mid-1950s: Exhaustion and a negotiated cessation of major hostilities; unresolved borders and grievances remain.
- Late 1950s–early 1960s: Uneven reconstruction, heavy censorship and state-directed industrial recovery.
- 1964: A bilateral transport accord creates the public cover for the C-17 mystery.

## Narrative and historical safeguards
- Do not present Nazism as benign, glamorous, or a viable political ideal. Monumental architecture should be contextualized as state power and coercion.
- No celebratory extremist insignia, slogans, or propaganda as collectibles or rewards. Historical imagery, if ever used, requires critical contextualization and legal review for target markets.
- Do not imply identical responsibility or identical historical crimes between the regimes. Show specific abuses accurately in their moral significance.
- Civilians in both countries have varied experiences; avoid national stereotypes.
- The game's central conspiracy is fictional, not a replacement for historical atrocities.

## Open for author review
The exact divergence point, armistice signatories, geopolitical borders and Germany's construction timeline need deliberate research and final approval. Do not silently fill gaps with claims of real history.
