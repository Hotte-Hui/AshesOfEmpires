# Game design document v0.1

## Player promise
Explore two ideologically divided postwar societies through two civilians who gradually learn that their inherited descriptions of the enemy do not capture the lived experience of ordinary people. Solve grounded mysteries, uncover records, and make choices with consequences.

## Core loop
Explore a small authored location → talk to NPCs and inspect objects → connect evidence → perform a contextual task or puzzle → make a dialogue or action decision → see persistent consequences → cinematic switch to the other protagonist.

## Scope guardrails
- First production target: a polished 20–30-minute vertical slice with both protagonists, one completed quest per character, one cinematic switch, save/load, and an ending hook.
- Long-term aspiration: six chapters and 8–12 hours; **not a promised deliverable** until proven by production estimates.
- Prefer compact, dense, authored districts over a vast empty open world.
- No combat system required for the vertical slice; investigation, exploration and character drama carry the game.

## Play modes
1. Side-on 2D: train compartments, intimate interiors, dialogue-heavy passages.
2. Oblique/isometric 2.5D: city streets, depots, industrial districts, NPC navigation.
3. Pixel-rendered 3D: monumental Berlin spaces, establishing shots, controlled camera motion.

## Character differentiation
**Alexei:** machinery, railway routing, damaged infrastructure, worker communities, physical-world clues.
**Matthias:** floorplans, restricted architectural records, bureaucracy, social tension, concealed spatial clues.

## Decisions
- Choices affect trust, evidence access, community support and who receives information.
- Do not use a simplistic morality meter or imply that repression is morally equivalent across regimes.
- Consequences should be visible in later conversations and locations.

## Accessibility
Rebindable inputs, subtitles, adjustable text size, reduced camera motion, optional pixel-shader intensity, high-contrast interaction highlights.
