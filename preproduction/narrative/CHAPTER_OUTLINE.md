# Six-chapter narrative outline

## 1. The Homecoming — Alexei
**Location:** Soviet rail depot and residential quarter. **Opening:** train enters a damaged industrial town at dawn. **Playable goal:** restore signal power and inspect a stalled cargo manifest. **Turn:** shipping numbers refer to a destination absent from public maps. **End image:** a stamped blueprint fragment under a flickering lamp.

## 2. The City of Stone — Matthias
**Location:** Berlin tram streets, design office, Great Hall perimeter. **Opening:** public ceremony contrasted with an eviction notice on a side street. **Playable goal:** reconcile contradictory foundation plans. **Turn:** an annex drawing uses the same routing code as Alexei's manifest. **End image:** the architect finds an unauthorized revision signed by his own office.

## 3. The Iron Peace — both, sequential
**Location:** neutral border conference town. **Opening:** Alexei arrives by rail; switch to Matthias arriving by official delegation. **Playable goals:** separate investigations of a vanished interpreter's notes. **Meeting:** they clash over the meaning of the evidence, then must cooperate to preserve a copy. **End:** each holds half of the key.

## 4. Voices Beneath Concrete — alternating
**Locations:** records rooms, tunnels, industrial plant. **Playable goals:** compare plans, trace redirected supplies, speak with displaced residents. **Turn:** the corridor has a concealed registry component. **Choice:** reveal a witness's identity or protect them at the cost of evidence access.

## 5. The Price of Peace — alternating
**Locations:** Berlin and Soviet industrial district. **Playable goals:** secure independent corroboration, repair critical civilian infrastructure, decide whom to trust. **Turn:** government factions attempt to seize the archive. **Consequences:** prior choices alter allies, access routes and public testimony.

## 6. Ashes and Dawn — alternating, convergent
**Locations:** border interchange and a final administrative chamber. **Playable goals:** choose a viable path for evidence and civilian protection. **Finale:** actions by both characters resolve the mystery. **Ending:** personalized epilogues for Alexei, Matthias and supporting communities.

## Character-switch design rule
Switches occur at story-authored points and preserve inventory, quest and relationship state. No unrestricted swap menu. A shared visual motif (plan, rail signal, announcement, light) links the scenes.
