# Vertical-slice scene and shot breakdown

## A1 — The Last Signal (Alexei)
**Opening:** Static wide shot of wet tracks, distant workers and a barely lit control cabin. 5–8 seconds; allow skip. **Player control:** Walk from platform to Irina, with three optional environmental observations. **Dialogue:** Irina states wagon counts are inconsistent; Alexei can ask about the timetable or the damaged seal. **Puzzle:** Connect three signal terminals in correct logical order using clues from an on-site diagram. Never require real-world railway safety knowledge. **Reveal:** Cargo ledger includes C-17 although the public map does not. **Choice:** Share discovery with Irina (`irina_trust +1`) or conceal it (`alexei_secrecy +1`). **Closing shot:** The C-17 stamp fills the frame, then sound bridge to tram bell.

## M1 — The Wrong Blueprint (Matthias)
**Opening:** Match cut from C-17 stamp to the corner of an architectural plan. **Player control:** Explore drafting office and archive. **Puzzle:** Overlay two fictional blueprints using visual landmarks; reveal an omitted service corridor marked C-17. **Dialogue:** Emil offers a warning about discrepancies. **Choice:** Preserve private evidence (`matthias_evidence +1`) or formally question the plans (`office_suspicion +1`). **Great Hall reveal:** Camera glides past an ordinary street and rises to the immense domed building, framing its oppressive scale rather than celebrating the regime. **Closing:** An apartment window closes as the hall's loudspeakers continue.

## X1 — Two Trains, One Record
**Intercut:** A radio announcement of limited diplomatic rail cooperation plays in two locations. Alexei and Matthias each mark the same route on different maps. **End:** Border route lights up on a neutral cartographic view. **Slice complete:** Acknowledgment of both choices, credits and return to menu. Do not fake a playable border chapter.
