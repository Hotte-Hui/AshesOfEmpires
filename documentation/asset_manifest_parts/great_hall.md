Every loader resolves through `Symbols.pick()` at build time (setting `historical_symbols`, CLI `--symbols=0|1`). The insignia are in-world iconography of the regime in a critical scene: the composition keeps the **evicted family** at the sealed door as the warm focal point, the regime's banner hangs over their sealed door, a neighbour draws her curtains and turns the light off. The **eagle above the globe** on the lantern follows Speer's real 1939 design for the Große Halle (the eagle originally planned above a swastika was changed to the globe) and carries no swastika, so it has no `_hist` variant. The Große Halle itself is fictional as a completed building (alternate 1964). |

## Verification (2026-11-04 evening, RTX 4090, Windows 11, 240 Hz display)
* Real-time harness runs at 642×362 in **both symbol modes**: frame delta avg 4.27–4.29 ms (vsync-bound ≈ 234 fps, p95 ≤ 4.8 ms; spikes 70–88 ms are the PNG-saving frames), viewport GPU 0.19 ms, viewport CPU 0.24–0.26 ms, 141 draw calls, ≈ 86 k primitives, **level update (camera + 102 sprites + life + halos) avg 0.64–0.66 ms, max 1.28 ms**.
* Palette: every captured frame (10 final frames + 6 story-beat frames) uses 35–36 colours, **0 pixels outside `pg.PALETTES['berlin']`**; every opaque texel of every generated symbol/life/sky/fog texture is a berlin colour.
* `--selftest` (headless): PASS (finished once for skip/skip and full play, all 7 cues in order, locale keys present, overrides, viewport restore). `tests/run_all.gd`: 17 files, 130 cases, ALL PASSED.
* Looked at in 3× crops: family + door banner + story window sequence (8.6 → 10.7 s), queue/sweeper/guard/steam, portico eagle and banners in both modes, lantern eagle, crane shot (pediments, kiosk, parked car, street banners), motion strips (walk cycles advance with distance, sweeper sweeps, tram departs, pigeons take off).

## Known limitations
* The generated citizens are background extras (36 px and smaller); the 46 px class uses the character sheets (`clerk`, `babushka`, family, `guard`) and follows their redesigns automatically. A 64 px foreground walker was tried and rejected (too crude at that size).
* The kiosk is only seen in the crane shot (h1); flying pigeons scale with perspective (no texel lock) while they fly away.
* Not run inside `Main.tscn` / `Game._cin_hall()` end-to-end in this round (autoplay/inputplay were not allowed while others work); verified in the harness at 640×360 and 642×362.
