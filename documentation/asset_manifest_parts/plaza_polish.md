## Mini-game

`scenes/ui/minigames/LeafletSnow.tscn` · `scripts/ui/minigames/leaflet_game.gd` (extends `minigame_base.gd`) +
`leaflet_logic.gd` (pure, deterministic `step(dt, dir, bend_held)`). Posts share a 16 s cycle (turn 1.1 s, sweep
3.1 s, three timing variants), calm window ≥ 5.8 s; gusts every 5.6 s from 4.2 s. Keys: ←/→ (A/D, pad) walk,
hold ↓ (S) or E/Space (pad A) to bend, Esc gives up; mouse: click the pavement to walk, hold on Konrad to bend.
Auto mode resolves after 0.2 s; `demo_solve()` uses synthetic key events only. Uses read-only: konrad and guard
`sheet.png/json`, Konrad's `portrait.png` (expression follows the situation).

## Generators and files

* `tools/pixelgen/gen_minigame_plaza.py` (`py -3 -P …`, `--preview <png>` optional) → `assets/ui/minigames/plaza/`
  at 1:1 art px, drawn ×3 nearest: `leaflet_back.png` 300×124 (sky, searchlights, colonnade, avenue, lane),
  `leaflet_march.png` 300×30 (tileable column, scrolls), `leaflet_barrier.png` 300×24, `leaflet_front.png` 300×124
  (crowd at the edges), `leaflet_bend.png` 3×40×56 (Konrad stooping, derived from `konrad/sheet.png` idle_side
  frame 0 — re-run when that sheet changes), `leaflet_sheet.png` 3×12×8, `world_leaflet.png` 2×8×6,
  `leaflet_layout.json`.
* `tools/pixelgen/explore_plaza.py leaflet frost_heart paws` → `assets/explore/plaza/{leaflet,frost_heart,paws}.png`
  (240×150 close-ups) and `assets/explore/plaza/world/paw_trail.png` (48×26 world decal).
* `tools/pixelgen/chargen_portrait2.py photographer` (spec `CAST['photographer']` in `chargen_portrait2_cast.py`:
  fedora with press card, dark gabardine coat, loosened tie, rangefinder camera on a strap) →
  `assets/characters/photographer/portrait.png` (4 expressions), `portrait_anim.png` (talk + blink rows),
  `portrait.json` — the chat speaker of `plaza.photographer` now has a face.
* Import afterwards under the shared lock (`.import_lock`).
